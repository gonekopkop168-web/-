របៀបលេង · HOW TO PLAY

ពាក់កណ្ដាលអធ្រាត្រ។ អ្នកភ្ញាក់ឡើងក្នុងផ្ទះឈើចាស់។ យកពិលនិងបង្កៃ (បង្កៃឫស្សីកោង មានខ្សែ និងដាក់ម្ហូប) ដើរតាមភ្លឺស្រែចង្អៀតៗ ដាក់បង្កៃ ៥ ក្នុងស្រែ ចាប់ត្រី ៣ ក្បាល រួចត្រឡប់ទៅផ្ទះឲ្យបានរស់។ នាងកំពុងនៅខាងក្រៅ។

It is midnight. You wake in an old wooden house. Take the flashlight and the បង្កៃ (a bent bamboo pole with a line and bait), walk the narrow rice-field dikes, place 5 traps in the paddies, catch 3 fish — then get back home alive. She is out there.

W A S D Move
Mouse Look
Shift Run (uses stamina)
Space Jump
E Interact / place trap / collect fish
Left Click Place fishing trap
F Flashlight
Esc Pause
  • Place traps only in the water between the rice (a green ghost-trap shows where).
  • Return later and press E at a trap to pull it out of the water. If a fish is inside, it joins your inventory and you get the trap back to place again.
  • Fish flee when you splash near them. Strange things happen on the fields: watch the shadows, the canals and your traps.
  • The house door can be closed – she cannot get through a closed door.
  • Running too long exhausts you. Batteries can be replaced at the table.
  • Phone: left stick = move, drag right side = look, buttons for run / jump / E (interact and place trap) / light.
